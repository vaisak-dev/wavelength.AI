Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear on nearly every first call.

  • How is Wavelength different from a traditional marketing agency?
    We treat answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) as a channel with the same rigor most agencies still reserve for Google — with dedicated measurement, not just SEO extended to a new acronym.
  • Do you work with early-stage companies or only enterprise brands?
    Both. Our Signal Audit adapts to budget and maturity — a seed-stage startup gets a leaner build than an enterprise account, but the same measurement discipline.
  • How long until we see results?
    Paid channels typically show movement within 2–4 weeks. Organic and AEO gains compound over 3–6 months, with most clients seeing measurable lift by month four.
  • Can you work alongside our in-house marketing team?
    Yes — most engagements plug directly into an existing team, filling specific gaps like AEO, paid media, or content production rather than replacing the whole function.
